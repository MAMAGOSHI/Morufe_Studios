Brand story

Named in honour of Morufe Ranala, grandmother of founder and director Andrew Nchabeleng, Morufe Studios was established in 2024 with a vision rooted in resilience, new beginnings and the possibilities that lie ahead.

We are a branding and design studio that uses research and strategic thinking to understand what makes a business distinct. From visual identity to motion, we develop design solutions that communicate with clarity and purpose.
