01 Brand story

Founded in 2024, Morufe Studios was inspired by the legacy of Morufe Ranala, grandmother of founder and director Andrew Nchabeleng, a symbol of new beginnings and resilience.

That legacy became the foundation of the studio. Morufe exists to empower businesses, enrich society and promote uniqueness through design.
